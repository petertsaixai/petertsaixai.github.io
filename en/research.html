Research

From connectivity to intelligent networks.

My research spans wireless communications, free-space optical communications, estimation and beam tracking, and emerging satellite and non-terrestrial networks.

FOUNDATION

Wireless communications

Early work included heterogeneous networks, NOMA, resource allocation, and high-altitude platform networks.

CORE DOCTORAL WORK

Free-space optical communications

Doctoral research focused on broadband wireless communication using FSO and radio frequency, including detector arrays, beam tracking, angle-of-arrival estimation, and statistical signal processing.

CURRENT DIRECTION

LEO, NTN & intelligent connectivity

Recent work extends toward low-Earth-orbit satellite communications and integrated aerial/space networks, connecting communications engineering with AI-enabled network optimisation.
